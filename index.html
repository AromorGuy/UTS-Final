Philosophical & Psychological Self

Module 2 Post-Assessment

Defining My Personal Philosophy: At this moment, I don’t believe there to be a defining moment so extreme that it shaped my beliefs about how to live. My main belief was built and shaped through many experiences, so I refuse to say that I can be defined by a single moment. Through multiple meaningful moments, be it good or bad, I’ve come to appreciate them and concluded that my great purpose in life is to experience.

Present and Future Self: I think I’ve found myself in a place where I still have so many options with what to do with my life and I’m glad I’m put in a position to be uncertain about my future. Opportunities are rare so I’d hope that I take every single one I'm interested in.

Real and Ideal Self: Currently, my ideal self is a student-athlete with top-notch grades. My real self is not too far from that, but I need so much more discipline. I am held back by the idea that what I’m doing is ok because I am comfortable, but realistically I’m incapable of being my ideal self with that mindset.

Part VIII

Physical & Sexual Self

Module 4 Post-Assessment

I view my physical self as something I need to care for and respect. However, most times I take for granted what my physical body allows me to do, ignoring the fact that I wouldn’t even be able to do the things I enjoy without being blessed with a healthy body. I must commit myself to healthier routines like eating balanced meals and resting when needed.

I understand my sexuality and gender identity and I believe that I am above letting anybody else but me define who I am. I stand with the belief that being able to act freely regardless of what others think is a sign of true confidence in one's own identity.

I believe body shaming, gender-based violence, and discrimination are serious issues that harm individuals’ confidence and safety. I can promote respect by treating others equally regardless of gender. And hopefully in my future profession, I can aim to create an environment where everyone feels valued regardless of gender.

Spiritual & Political Self

Module 6 Post-Assessment

Political Self: I’m not very political, but my values are all the same, it's to ultimately “be kind” and I need to see others reflect that value in some form. Social media has significantly influenced my political beliefs, particularly seeing younger generations speaking out. The only political belief I have is that I’d never forgive people in power abusing their power in ways that cause harm to others.

Spiritual Self: I am a Christian Catholic but I’m not religious whatsoever. However, I think it’s always good to have something to believe in. As much as possible I’d like to just go with the flow with things and react accordingly.

Even though I’m not very religious, I believe that faith isn’t just about prayer or believing in a higher being but rather about trusting that things will work out in time. I remember not thinking our hackathon idea would be anywhere close to winning even in the Top 3 but we turned out to be the Top 1 somehow and I’m really grateful for that.
